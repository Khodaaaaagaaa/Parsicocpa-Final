Frequently asked questions

I received an IRS CP2000 — what should I do?

A CP2000 proposes changes based on income the IRS believes was underreported. It has a response deadline. We can review it, compare it to your records, and prepare a timely response.

Do you handle California FTB notices too?

Yes. We work with both IRS and California Franchise Tax Board notices, audits, and collection matters.

Can you represent me before the IRS?

We provide representation on eligible tax matters under the supervision of a licensed CPA, based on your specific situation.
